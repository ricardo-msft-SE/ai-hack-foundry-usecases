# City of Exampleville — Services Overview

This document provides official information about city services available to Exampleville residents.

---

## Benefits & Assistance Programs

### Food Assistance (SNAP)

Exampleville partners with the State Department of Social Services to provide SNAP (Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program) benefits to eligible households.

**Eligibility:**
- Household gross income at or below 130% of the federal poverty level
- Must be a city resident with a valid government-issued ID
- Non-citizens may qualify depending on immigration status — contact the Benefits Office for guidance

**How to Apply:**
- Online: benefits.exampleville.gov
- In-person: City Benefits Office, 123 Main Street, Mon–Fri 8am–5pm
- By phone: 311

**Processing Time:** 30 days for standard applications; 7 days for expedited cases (households with no income or less than $150 in resources)

**Benefit Amount:** Based on household size and income. Average benefit: $200–$400/month for a family of 4.

---

### Utility Assistance (HEAP)

The Home Energy Assistance Program (HEAP) helps eligible households pay their heating and cooling bills.

**Eligibility:**
- Household income at or below 60% of the state median income
- Must occupy the residence as your primary home

**Application Period:** November 1 – March 31 each year (cooling assistance: June 1 – August 31)

**Benefits:** Up to $500 per season for heating; up to $200 for cooling emergencies

**How to Apply:** Apply online at benefits.exampleville.gov or call 311.

---

### Unemployment Benefits

Exampleville administers state unemployment insurance for residents who lost their jobs through no fault of their own.

**Eligibility:**
- Worked at least 20 weeks in the past base year
- Earned at least $2,000 in the base period
- Actively seeking new employment (must document job search activities weekly)

**How to Apply:**
- Online: unemployment.exampleville.gov
- By phone: (555) 234-5678, Mon–Fri 8am–4pm

**Benefit Amount:** 60% of your average weekly wage, up to $504/week, for up to 26 weeks

---

### Housing Assistance

The city provides emergency rental assistance and transitional housing support.

**Emergency Rental Assistance:**
- Up to 3 months of back rent for households at risk of eviction
- Income must be at or below 80% of Area Median Income (AMI)
- Apply at: housing.exampleville.gov

**Transitional Housing:** Contact the Housing Authority at (555) 345-6789 for shelter placements.

## Permits & Licenses

### Business License

All businesses operating within city limits must obtain a City Business License before opening.

**Requirements:**
- Completed application form (available online)
- Proof of business address (lease agreement or deed)
- Payment of $150 filing fee (non-refundable)
- Zoning compliance letter (required for physical storefronts — contact Planning Department)
- State sales tax ID (for businesses selling goods)

**Processing Time:** 5–10 business days after a complete application is submitted

**Renewal:** Annual, due by January 31. Late fee: $50.

---

### Building & Construction Permit

Required for any structural modification, addition, new construction, or demolition.

**Requirements:**
- Architectural plans (must be stamped by a licensed architect for projects over $25,000)
- Site plan showing property boundaries and setbacks
- Payment of permit fee (see schedule below)

**Fee Schedule:**
- Projects up to $10,000: $75
- Projects $10,001–$50,000: $150
- Projects over $50,000: 0.5% of total project value

**Required Inspections:** Foundation, framing, electrical rough-in, plumbing rough-in, final

**Processing Time:** 10–15 business days for residential; 20–30 days for commercial

---

### Special Event Permit

Required for any public event with 50 or more attendees on city property (streets, parks, plazas).

**Lead Time:** Apply at least 30 days before the event date

**Requirements:**
- Detailed event description and site layout map
- Proof of liability insurance ($1 million minimum per occurrence)
- Noise variance application (if amplified music after 10pm)
- Security plan (for events over 500 attendees)

**Fee:** $100 base fee + $50 per 100 attendees over 100

## Schedules

### Garbage & Recycling Collection

**Garbage Collection:** Weekly, every week of the year including most holidays.

Place your bins at the curb **by 7:00 AM** on your collection day. Late bins may be missed.

**Recycling Collection:** Every other week (alternating with yard waste).

**Accepted Recyclables:**
- Paper and cardboard (flattened)
- Glass bottles and jars (rinsed)
- Plastic containers #1 through #7
- Aluminum and steel cans

**NOT Accepted in Recycling:**
- Plastic bags or film wrap
- Food waste or soiled containers
- Electronics (see e-waste events below)
- Batteries, paint, or chemicals (see Household Hazardous Waste program)

**Bulk Item Pickup:** First Saturday of every month. Schedule online at trash.exampleville.gov or call 311 by the Thursday prior.

**Holiday Schedule:** Collection is delayed by one day when a federal holiday falls Monday through Thursday. No delay for Friday holidays.

---

### Street Cleaning

Street cleaning occurs on alternating weeks per block.

- **Side A (odd-numbered addresses):** Tuesdays, 8:00 AM – 11:00 AM
- **Side B (even-numbered addresses):** Thursdays, 8:00 AM – 11:00 AM

Vehicles parked on the street during cleaning will be ticketed. **Fine: $65.**

Suspensions (no-ticket days): All federal holidays, declared snow emergencies, and city-declared emergencies.

## Emergency Services

### Emergency Alerts

Sign up for Exampleville Emergency Alerts at alerts.exampleville.gov. Alerts are sent by text, email, and phone.

**Alert Types:** Severe weather warnings, road closures, public health emergencies, Amber Alerts, mandatory evacuations

**Emergency:** Call **911** for life-threatening emergencies.
**Non-Emergency City Services:** Call **311** or use the 311 app.

## Transportation & Parking

### Residential Parking Permits

Residents in designated parking permit zones must display a valid permit.

**Cost:** $40/year per vehicle (up to 2 vehicles per household)

**How to Apply:** Online at parking.exampleville.gov or in person at City Hall, Room 101

**Visitor Passes:** 10 temporary passes (24-hour) included with each annual permit. Additional passes: $2 each.

**Required Documents:** Valid driver's license, vehicle registration, proof of residency (utility bill or lease)

---

### Public Transit

The Exampleville Transit Authority (ETA) operates bus and light rail service throughout the city.

**Day Pass:** $5 (unlimited rides)
**Monthly Pass:** $85
**Reduced Fare:** Available for seniors (65+), people with disabilities, and low-income residents

Information: rideeta.com or call (555) 456-7890

## Public Health

### Vaccination Clinics

Free vaccination clinics are held at City Health Centers on the first and third Tuesday of each month.

**Vaccines Available:** Flu, COVID-19, Tdap, Hepatitis A & B, and childhood immunizations

**Locations:**
- North Health Center: 456 Oak Avenue, 9am–3pm
- South Health Center: 789 Pine Road, 10am–4pm

No appointment necessary. Bring your insurance card if you have one (uninsured patients are served at no cost).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I find out my trash collection day?**
A: Enter your address at trash.exampleville.gov or call 311.

**Q: Can I apply for a business license online?**
A: Yes, fully online applications are accepted at biz.exampleville.gov. Processing takes 5–10 business days.

**Q: What is the income limit for food assistance (SNAP)?**
A: For a family of 4, the gross monthly income limit is approximately $3,007 (130% of the federal poverty level, updated annually).

**Q: How long does it take to get a building permit?**
A: Standard residential permits take 10–15 business days after a complete application is submitted. Commercial permits take 20–30 days.

**Q: How do I report a pothole or broken streetlight?**
A: Use the Exampleville 311 app, call 311, or report online at 311.exampleville.gov. Include the address and a photo if possible.

**Q: Is there help with my utility bills?**
A: Yes — the HEAP program assists with heating and cooling bills. Apply at benefits.exampleville.gov during the application period (Nov 1–Mar 31 for heating).

**Q: How do I get a parking permit for my street?**
A: Apply online at parking.exampleville.gov. You'll need your driver's license, vehicle registration, and a proof of residency document. Cost is $40/year per vehicle.
