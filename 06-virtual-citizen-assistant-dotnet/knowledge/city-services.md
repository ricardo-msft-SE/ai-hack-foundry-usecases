# City of Exampleville — City Services Guide

Official guide to permits, licenses, schedules, and programs for residents and businesses.

---

## Building Permits

### When You Need a Building Permit

A building permit is required for:
- New construction of any structure
- Additions or extensions to existing structures
- Renovation or alteration that changes the structural elements, plumbing, or electrical systems
- Installation of new HVAC equipment
- Demolition of structures larger than a storage shed

A building permit is NOT required for:
- Painting, wallpapering, or floor refinishing
- Cabinet installation (no structural changes)
- Replacing fixtures (same type and location)
- Fences under 4 feet high in residential zones

### How to Apply for a Building Permit

1. Create an account at permits.exampleville.gov
2. Submit a permit application including:
   - Site plan (drawn to scale)
   - Construction drawings (stamped by a licensed engineer or architect for projects over $50,000)
   - Contractor information (must be licensed in the city)
   - Proof of property ownership or authorization letter from owner
3. Pay the application fee (calculated by project value)
4. Wait for plan review (5–15 business days depending on complexity)
5. Permit is issued digitally — print or display on mobile device at job site

### Building Permit Fees

| Project Value | Permit Fee |
|---|---|
| Under $5,000 | $75 base fee |
| $5,000–$49,999 | $75 + 1.5% of project value |
| $50,000–$499,999 | $75 + 1.2% of project value |
| $500,000+ | $75 + 1.0% of project value |

### Required Inspections

After the permit is issued, these inspections are typically required:
1. **Foundation inspection** — before pouring concrete
2. **Rough framing inspection** — before walls are closed
3. **Rough electrical and plumbing** — before walls are closed
4. **Insulation** — before drywall
5. **Final inspection** — after all work is complete

Schedule inspections at permits.exampleville.gov or call (555) 234-5678.

## Business Licenses

### General Business License

All businesses operating within city limits must hold a valid City Business License, renewed annually by January 31.

**Fees by Business Type:**
| Category | Annual Fee |
|---|---|
| Home-based business | $50 |
| Small retail/service (under $100K revenue) | $150 |
| Medium retail/service ($100K–$1M revenue) | $300 |
| Large business ($1M+ revenue) | $500 |
| Contractor (licensed) | $200 |

### Special Business Licenses

Some business types require additional licenses beyond the general business license:

- **Food Service Permit** — all restaurants, food carts, catering businesses
- **Liquor License** — apply through the State Liquor Authority (city issues a separate local permit: $250/year)
- **Sidewalk Café Permit** — restaurants using sidewalk seating: $350/year + $100 per table
- **Pawnshop License** — $500/year + security requirements
- **Towing Company License** — $400/year

### Applying for a Business License

1. Visit business.exampleville.gov
2. Provide: business name, owner name, business address, EIN, business type
3. Submit to the Zoning department for a zoning compliance check ($0 — included in the business license fee)
4. If zoning is approved, pay the annual fee
5. License is emailed within 3 business days

## Food Service Permits

All food service operations require a valid Food Service Permit (administered jointly with the Department of Public Health).

### Requirements
- Valid City Business License (above)
- Completed food manager certification for at least one employee (ServSafe or equivalent)
- Facility inspection passed before permit is issued (call (555) 678-9012 to schedule)
- Operating under an approved facility plan (kitchen layout, equipment list)

### Permit Fees
| Type | Annual Fee |
|---|---|
| Food cart / mobile unit | $150 |
| Small restaurant (under 50 seats) | $250 |
| Large restaurant (50+ seats) | $400 |
| Food manufacturer | $600 |

## Event Permits

### When an Event Permit is Required

An event permit is required for any public or semi-public event:
- Event on a public street, sidewalk, or park
- Event with 50 or more attendees
- Event with amplified sound outdoors
- Event serving alcohol in a public space
- Parade, procession, or march

### Application Lead Times

| Event Size | Lead Time |
|---|---|
| Under 100 people, park event | 14 days |
| 100–499 people | 30 days |
| 500–4,999 people | 60 days |
| 5,000+ people | 90 days |

### Event Permit Fees

- Under 100 attendees: $50
- 100–499 attendees: $150
- 500–4,999 attendees: $400
- 5,000+ attendees: $750 + cost recovery for police/sanitation

## Trash and Recycling

### Collection Schedules

Collection schedules vary by neighborhood. Look up your address at sanitation.exampleville.gov/schedule.

**General schedule by area:**
| Area | Trash Pickup | Recycling |
|---|---|---|
| North Exampleville | Monday & Thursday | Wednesday |
| South Exampleville | Tuesday & Friday | Thursday |
| East Exampleville | Monday & Wednesday | Friday |
| West Exampleville | Tuesday & Saturday | Monday |
| Downtown | Daily (Mon–Sat) | Tuesday & Friday |

### What Goes in Each Bin

**Recycling (Blue bin):**
- Paper, cardboard (flattened)
- Glass bottles and jars
- Plastic containers #1–7 (rinsed)
- Metal cans and aluminum foil

**Trash (Gray bin):**
- Food waste (or use the Green organics bin if provided)
- Non-recyclable plastics, foam
- Broken glass

**Bulk items:** Schedule a bulk pickup at sanitation.exampleville.gov/bulk. Free for residential — up to 4 items per request.

## Street Cleaning

Street cleaning operates on alternating days, usually between 8:00 AM – 11:00 AM.

**Before parking your car:** Check the posted street cleaning signs. Moving your car before the posted time avoids a $65 ticket.

Alternate-side parking is suspended on all official city holidays. Check the holiday schedule at parking.exampleville.gov.
