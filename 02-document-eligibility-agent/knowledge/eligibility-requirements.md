# City of Exampleville — Benefits Eligibility Requirements

This document contains the official eligibility requirements, income limits, and required documentation for all city-administered benefit programs.

---

## SNAP (Food Assistance)

### Income Limits (Effective October 2024 – September 2025)

| Household Size | Gross Monthly Income Limit (130% FPL) | Net Monthly Income Limit (100% FPL) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $1,580 | $1,215 |
| 2 | $2,137 | $1,644 |
| 3 | $2,694 | $2,072 |
| 4 | $3,250 | $2,500 |
| 5 | $3,807 | $2,928 |
| Each additional member | +$557 | +$429 |

### Required Documents

- **Proof of identity:** State-issued ID, driver's license, passport, or birth certificate
- **Proof of residency:** Utility bill (within 60 days), lease agreement, or postmarked mail
- **Proof of income:** W-2 (most recent), last 2 pay stubs, or letter from employer
  - Self-employed: Most recent federal tax return (Schedule C)
  - No income: Signed statement explaining situation
- **Proof of resources:** Bank statements (within 30 days) if household has liquid assets
- **Social Security numbers** for all household members applying

### Document Age Requirements

- Pay stubs: Must be within **30 days** of application date
- Bank statements: Must be within **30 days** of application date
- Utility bills: Must be within **60 days** of application date
- Tax returns: Prior year's return is acceptable

### Categorical Eligibility

Households receiving SSI, TANF, or General Assistance are **categorically eligible** — income verification is not required. Only identity and residency verification needed.

## HEAP (Utility Assistance)

### Income Limits (Annual, Effective 2024–2025)

| Household Size | 60% State Median Income (Annual) |
|---|---|
| 1 | $36,482 |
| 2 | $47,645 |
| 3 | $58,808 |
| 4 | $69,972 |
| Each additional member | +$11,163 |

### Required Documents

- **Proof of identity:** Government-issued photo ID
- **Proof of residency:** Must occupy the unit as primary residence (lease, utility bill, or mortgage statement)
- **Proof of income:** Last 4 pay stubs **or** most recent W-2 for all household members over 18
  - Social Security: Award letter or SSA-1099
  - Pension/retirement: Most recent pension statement
- **Utility account information:** Account number and utility provider name (electric, gas, or heating oil)
- **Landlord certification:** Required if heat is included in rent

### Benefit Types

- **Regular HEAP:** One-time benefit per season, credited directly to utility account
- **Emergency HEAP:** For households facing shutoff in 15 days or disconnected. Requires shutoff notice.
- **Heating Equipment Repair/Replace:** For broken heating equipment. Requires contractor estimate.

## Unemployment Benefits

### Eligibility Criteria

- **Employment history:** Worked at least 20 weeks in the base year (52 weeks prior to claim)
- **Earnings:** Earned at least $2,000 in the base period
- **Separation reason:** Laid off, reduced hours, or left for good cause (not voluntary quit without cause)
- **Availability:** Able to work, available for work, and actively seeking employment
- **Weekly certification:** Must certify job search activities (minimum 3 contacts/week)

### Required Documents

- **Proof of identity:** State-issued ID or passport
- **Employment history:** W-2s for the past 2 years, or employer name/address/dates of employment
- **Separation documentation:** Layoff letter, severance agreement, or employer contact information
- **Banking information:** Direct deposit form (checking or savings account)

### Benefit Calculation

- **Weekly benefit rate:** 60% of average weekly wage during the base period
- **Minimum benefit:** $104/week
- **Maximum benefit:** $504/week
- **Duration:** Up to 26 weeks; may extend to 39 weeks during high unemployment periods

### Disqualifying Factors

- Voluntary resignation without good cause
- Termination for misconduct
- Refusal of suitable work
- Failure to report earnings from part-time work
- Out-of-state for work or unavailable for work

## Housing Assistance (Emergency Rental Assistance)

### Income Limits

Household income must be at or below **80% of the Area Median Income (AMI)**:

| Household Size | 80% AMI (Annual) |
|---|---|
| 1 | $52,800 |
| 2 | $60,350 |
| 3 | $67,900 |
| 4 | $75,400 |

### Eligibility Requirements

- Must be a renter (not a homeowner)
- Must demonstrate risk of housing instability: eviction notice, court filing, or landlord letter
- Must not have received emergency rental assistance in the past 12 months from any source
- Landlord must agree to participate and sign a landlord certification

### Required Documents

**From Applicant:**
- Government-issued photo ID
- Proof of residency (lease agreement — full signed copy required)
- Proof of income (last 2 pay stubs, W-2, or benefit award letters)
- Proof of housing instability (eviction notice, court summons, or landlord letter)
- Bank statements (last 30 days) — only if applying for security deposit assistance

**From Landlord:**
- Signed landlord certification form
- W-9 tax form
- Ledger showing rent owed and payment history

### Benefit Amount

- Up to **3 months** of back rent per application
- Up to **2 months** of prospective rent (to prevent future eviction)
- Security deposit: Up to 1.5 months' rent for households moving to prevent homelessness

## Document Validation Rules (All Programs)

### Acceptable Proof of Income — Priority Order

1. **W-2 form** (most recent tax year) — highest confidence
2. **Last 2 consecutive pay stubs** — must show employer name, pay period, and gross pay
3. **Employer verification letter** — on company letterhead, signed, with salary and employment dates
4. **Most recent federal tax return** — for self-employed or irregular income
5. **Benefit award letter** — for SSI, SSDI, pension, or child support income
6. **Self-attestation** — only acceptable if applicant has no income; requires signed affidavit

### Documents That Are NOT Acceptable

- Screenshots of bank transfers or Venmo/PayPal transactions (as sole income proof)
- Pay stubs without employer name or pay period
- Documents older than specified limits
- Documents with visible alterations or inconsistencies
- Photocopies of photocopies (unclear copies that cannot be verified)

### Red Flags — Flag for Supervisor Review

- Income on W-2 does not match income on pay stubs for same employer
- Address on ID does not match address on residency proof
- Document dates are inconsistent (pay stub dated after W-2 filing date)
- Same document appears to have been reused from a prior application
- Signatures on forms appear different from signatures on ID
