## SNAP (Food Assistance)

### Gross Income Limits (2024–2025)

| Household Size | Monthly Gross Limit | Monthly Net Limit |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | $1,580 | $1,215 |
| 2 | $2,137 | $1,644 |
| 3 | $2,694 | $2,072 |
| 4 | $3,250 | $2,500 |
| Each add'l | +$557 | +$429 |

### Categorical Eligibility
Households receiving TANF, SSI, or General Assistance are categorically eligible — simplified documentation required.

### Application and Renewal
- Applications processed within 30 days (7 days for expedited)
- Certification period: 6–12 months
- Renewal requires re-interview (phone or in-person)
- Lost/stolen EBT card: Call (555) 123-4567 immediately; replacement issued within 5 business days

## TANF (Temporary Assistance for Needy Families)

### Purpose
Provides temporary cash assistance to families with children when income is insufficient to meet basic needs.

### Eligibility
- Household with a child under 18 (or 19 if full-time student)
- Income and resources below program limits
- Must comply with work requirements (exceptions for disability, caregiving, domestic violence)

### Work Requirements (Federal)
- Single parents with child 6+: 30 hours/week of work activities
- Two-parent households: 35 hours/week combined
- Work activities include: employment, job training, education, community service

### Benefit Amount
Cash grant based on family size and income:
- Family of 1: up to $229/month
- Family of 2: up to $367/month
- Family of 3: up to $488/month
- Family of 4: up to $607/month

### Time Limits
Federal lifetime limit: 60 months (5 years) of TANF assistance. State may provide additional assistance beyond this limit using state-only funds.

## Emergency Rental Assistance

### Eligibility
- Household income at or below 80% of Area Median Income (AMI)
- Renter (not homeowner)
- Demonstrable risk of housing instability: eviction notice, court filing, arrears
- Has not received emergency rental assistance in the past 12 months

### Maximum Benefit
- Up to 3 months of back rent
- Up to 2 months of prospective (future) rent
- Security deposit: up to 1.5 months' rent (for households avoiding homelessness)

### Landlord Participation
Landlord must agree to participate, sign a certification, and provide a W-9. Payments are made directly to the landlord.

### Application Timeline
Processing target: 30 days. Emergency processing (facing eviction within 14 days): 7 days.

## Child Care Subsidy

### Purpose
Helps eligible families afford licensed child care while parents work, attend school, or participate in job training.

### Eligibility
- Child must be under 13 years old
- Household income at or below 200% FPL
- Parent/guardian must be working, in school, or in job training (minimum 20 hours/week)

### Benefit
Subsidy covers the difference between the family co-payment and the maximum reimbursement rate for licensed providers.

**Family Co-Payment Schedule:**
| Income (% FPL) | Co-Pay per Week |
|---|---|
| Under 100% | $0 |
| 100–150% | $15 |
| 150–175% | $25 |
| 175–200% | $40 |

### Approved Providers
Only licensed child care centers and registered family day care providers are eligible. Verify provider status at childcare.exampleville.gov/providers.

## Domestic Violence Services

### Confidentiality
All contacts with domestic violence service programs are strictly confidential. Information is not shared with other agencies without explicit consent from the individual, except as required by law.

### Services Available
- Emergency shelter placement (24-hour hotline: 1-800-799-SAFE)
- Safety planning assistance
- Civil legal assistance (protective orders)
- Transitional housing
- Economic empowerment programs

### Benefits while in Domestic Violence Situation
- Good cause exemption from TANF work requirements
- Address confidentiality program — benefits can be received without disclosing address
- Expedited processing for all benefit applications

## Inter-Agency Protocols

### Social Services + Department of Labor
Joint screening protocol: All unemployment claimants are offered SNAP pre-screening. Workers are co-enrolled where eligible. Protocol JP-LAB-SS-2023.

### Social Services + Public Health
Medicaid is co-administered with the Public Health Department. Applications may be filed jointly. Protocol JP-SS-PH-2021.

### Social Services + DMV
Identity verification partnership: Social Services staff may request DMV identity verification for benefits applicants without requiring the applicant to go to the DMV in person. Data Sharing Agreement DSA-2023-04.

### Social Services + General Services (Procurement)
All vendors providing direct services (child care providers, shelters, food banks) must be registered in the city vendor system administered by General Services. Contract compliance is monitored jointly.
