## Procurement Policy

### Competitive Bidding Thresholds

| Purchase Amount | Required Process |
|---|---|
| Under $2,500 | No competitive bidding required; department purchase order |
| $2,500–$9,999 | Minimum 3 telephone or email quotes required |
| $10,000–$49,999 | Minimum 3 written quotes required |
| $50,000–$149,999 | Request for Proposals (RFP) — advertise for 2 weeks minimum |
| $150,000+ | Formal competitive sealed bid — advertise for 30 days minimum |

### Sole Source Procurement

Competitive bidding may be waived only when:
1. There is only one vendor capable of providing the goods/services
2. Emergency procurement is required (threat to public health or safety)
3. The purchase is from another government entity under cooperative purchasing

**Sole source requests must be approved by the Procurement Director** and documented in the procurement file.

### Vendor Registration

All vendors doing business with the city must register in the City Vendor Portal (vendor.exampleville.gov) before receiving a purchase order.

**Registration Requirements:**
- Business name, address, and contact information
- Federal Employer Identification Number (FEIN) or SSN for sole proprietors
- W-9 tax form
- Certificate of insurance (if providing services on city property)
- MBE/WBE certification (if applicable — voluntary, for tracking diversity goals)

**Annual Registration Fee:** None. Registration is free and renewable every 2 years.

## Contracts

### Contract Types

| Contract Type | Description | Max Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Purchase Order (PO) | One-time purchase of goods | N/A |
| Service Contract | Recurring services | 3 years (with up to 2 one-year renewals) |
| Construction Contract | Capital projects | Project duration |
| Professional Services | Legal, consulting, engineering | 2 years (with renewals) |

### Contract Modification

Changes to existing contracts (scope, cost, timeline) require a formal amendment:
- Under 10% increase in contract value: Department head approval
- 10–25% increase: Procurement Director approval
- Over 25% increase: City Council approval required

### Performance Bonds

Required for construction contracts over $100,000. Bond must be 100% of contract value from a surety company licensed in the state.

## Facilities Management

### City-Owned Buildings

General Services manages all city-owned buildings (47 buildings, 1.2M square feet total).

**Service Requests:**
- Submit via the Facility Management Portal (facilities.exampleville.gov) or call (555) 890-1234
- Emergency repairs (leaks, no heat in winter, structural): 4-hour response
- Urgent repairs (HVAC malfunction, elevator outage): Next business day
- Standard repairs: Within 5 business days

### Space Allocation

Departments requesting additional space or new office assignments must submit a Space Request Form to General Services at least 90 days in advance.

**Approval Process:**
1. Department submits request (current headcount, space needed, timeline)
2. Facilities team assesses options (30 days)
3. Department Director and General Services Director approve
4. Move/build-out scheduled

## IT Standards

### Approved Software List

All software must be on the Approved Software List before being installed on city systems. Contact the IT Standards Division to request review of new software.

**Fast-Track Approval:** Pre-approved Microsoft 365, Azure, and other enterprise agreements.

### Cloud Procurement Standards

All cloud services must meet these minimum requirements:
- **Data residency:** Data must remain in the United States (or a documented exception)
- **Security certifications:** SOC 2 Type II or FedRAMP Moderate (minimum)
- **Encryption:** Data at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.2+)
- **Access logging:** Comprehensive audit logs retained for 7 years
- **DPA:** Vendor must sign the city's Data Processing Addendum

### AI and Generative AI Procurement

Purchases of AI tools and services must comply with the City AI Governance Policy (AI-POL-2024):
- AI Risk Assessment required before procurement (template at IT.exampleville.gov)
- High-risk AI (benefits decisions, enforcement) requires City Council notification
- All AI vendors must agree to the City AI Addendum covering transparency, bias testing, and data use
- Annual review of all AI contracts by the AI Governance Committee

### Acceptable Use Policy

City IT resources (computers, network, email, cloud services) are for official city business.

**Prohibited activities:**
- Storing personal files or data on city systems
- Using city email for personal communications (incidental personal use is permitted; volume must be minimal)
- Accessing, downloading, or sharing pornographic, violent, or discriminatory content
- Using city accounts to access personal social media for non-work purposes

Violations may result in disciplinary action including termination.

## Inter-Agency Coordination

### General Services + Social Services
All vendors providing social services (child care providers, food banks, shelter operators) must be registered in the city vendor system before contracts are issued. Joint vendor management protocol: JP-SS-GS-2023.

### General Services + IT (All Departments)
The IT Division within General Services provides technology procurement support to all city agencies. Department IT needs should be routed through IT Standards before issuing RFPs.

### General Services + OEM (Emergency Procurement)
Emergency procurement procedures allow waiving competitive bidding during declared city emergencies. OEM Director must declare emergency and notify General Services. Documentation must be completed within 15 days of emergency procurement. Protocol EOP-Procurement-2022.
