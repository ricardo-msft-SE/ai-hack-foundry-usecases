## Driver's Licenses

### Standard Driver's License (Class D)

**Eligibility:**
- Must be 17 years of age or older (16 with completed driver's education)
- Must pass vision test (20/40 corrected, or 20/70 with restriction)
- Must pass written knowledge test (passing score: 70%)
- Must pass road skills test

**Required Documents (REAL ID compliant):**
- Proof of identity: Valid U.S. passport, birth certificate, or certificate of citizenship
- Proof of Social Security number: Social Security card, W-2, or pay stub with full SSN
- Two proofs of residency: Utility bills, bank statements, or lease within 60 days

**Fees:**
- New license (Class D): $80 (8-year term)
- Renewal: $64
- Duplicate: $30

### Commercial Driver's License (CDL)

CDLs are required for drivers of vehicles over 26,001 lbs, vehicles carrying 16+ passengers, or any hazardous materials transport.

**CDL Classes:**
| Class | Vehicle Type |
|---|---|
| Class A | Combination vehicles (tractor-trailers, doubles, triples) |
| Class B | Single vehicles over 26,001 lbs (buses, dump trucks, garbage trucks) |
| Class C | Vehicles carrying 16–passenger+ or hazmat (vans, small buses) |

**Endorsements:**
- H — Hazardous Materials (requires TSA background check, $86 fee)
- P — Passenger Vehicles
- S — School Bus
- T — Double/Triple Trailers
- N — Tank Vehicles
- X — Combination Tank/Hazmat

**Medical Requirements:** All CDL holders must maintain a valid FMCSA Medical Examiner's Certificate.

**Disqualifications:** DUI/DWI, refusing chemical test, leaving the scene of an accident while driving a commercial vehicle, using a vehicle to commit a felony.

## Vehicle Registration

### Standard Registration

**Annual Renewal Requirements:**
- Valid insurance (minimum state liability: 25/50/10)
- Passing emissions inspection (vehicles 3+ years old)
- Payment of registration fee

**Fees (based on vehicle weight):**
| Vehicle Weight | Annual Fee |
|---|---|
| Under 1,500 lbs | $40 |
| 1,500–2,499 lbs | $65 |
| 2,500–3,499 lbs | $85 |
| 3,500+ lbs | $110 |

### Commercial Vehicle Registration

**Additional Requirements:**
- Commercial vehicle permit (for vehicles over 26,001 lbs)
- International Registration Plan (IRP) for vehicles crossing state lines
- USDOT number for interstate commerce
- Unified Carrier Registration (UCR) for interstate motor carriers

## License Reinstatement

### Suspended License Reinstatement

**Common Suspension Reasons and Reinstatement:**

| Reason | Suspension Period | Reinstatement Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| DUI (first offense) | 6 months | $100 fee, alcohol education program, SR-22 insurance |
| DUI (second offense) | 1–5 years | $200 fee, ignition interlock for 1 year, SR-22 insurance |
| Accumulation of 11+ points | 90 days | $50 fee, safe driver course |
| Failure to pay traffic fine | Until paid | Pay all outstanding fines + $50 reinstatement fee |
| Failure to maintain insurance | Until proof provided | Provide proof of insurance + $50 reinstatement fee |

**SR-22 Insurance:** Certificate of financial responsibility required for high-risk drivers. Must be maintained for 3 years.

## Inter-Agency Coordination

### DMV + Social Services Data Sharing
The DMV shares identity verification data with the Social Services Department under Data Sharing Agreement #DSA-2023-04 to support benefits eligibility verification. Staff may request identity verification through the Joint Verification Portal.

### DMV + Department of Labor (Commercial Vehicles)
Employers who require CDL drivers may verify license status and endorsements through the Commercial Driver Verification API (staff portal login required).

### Suspended License and Employment
If a resident's license is suspended and it affects their employment, Social Services may provide emergency transportation assistance while the license reinstatement process is completed.
