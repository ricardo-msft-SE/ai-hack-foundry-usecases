## Unemployment Insurance

### Eligibility Requirements

- **Work history:** Minimum 20 weeks of employment in the base year
- **Earnings:** Minimum $2,000 earned during the base period
- **Separation reason:** Must have lost job through no fault of their own (layoff, reduction in hours, or constructive dismissal)
- **Availability:** Must be physically able to work, available for work, and actively seeking employment
- **Weekly job search:** Minimum 3 documented job contacts per week required for continued eligibility

### Benefit Calculation

- **Weekly Benefit Rate (WBR):** 60% of average weekly wage during base period
- **Minimum WBR:** $104/week
- **Maximum WBR:** $504/week
- **Duration:** 26 weeks standard; up to 39 weeks during high-unemployment periods (>8% state unemployment rate)

### Disqualifying Events

- Voluntary resignation without good cause (good cause includes: domestic violence, medical necessity, significant reduction in pay/hours)
- Termination for misconduct (defined as willful disregard of employer's reasonable expectations)
- Refusal of suitable work without good reason
- Failure to participate in re-employment services when required
- Fraud or misrepresentation on the application

### Overpayment and Fraud

- Overpayments must be repaid; interest accrues at 12% per year after 30 days
- Fraudulent claims result in disqualification for 1 year + repayment of all fraudulent amounts + potential criminal prosecution

## Workplace Safety

### Worker Rights

- Right to a workplace free from recognized hazards
- Right to report unsafe conditions without retaliation
- Right to request OSHA inspection
- Right to receive safety training in a language they understand
- Right to see employer's OSHA 300 log of work-related injuries

### Employer Obligations

- Must post the "Worker Rights" poster in a visible location
- Must report all workplace fatalities within 8 hours
- Must report all in-patient hospitalizations, amputations, or loss of an eye within 24 hours
- Must maintain OSHA 300 log for all recordable injuries and illnesses

### Filing a Workplace Complaint

Complaints can be filed:
- Online: safety.exampleville.gov
- By phone: (555) 567-8901
- In person: Department of Labor Office, 234 Commerce Street

All complaints are investigated within 30 business days. Complainant identity is kept confidential upon request.

## Wage Claims

### Filing a Wage Theft Claim

Workers who have not been paid wages owed (including overtime, tips, or final paycheck) may file a wage claim with the Department of Labor.

**Time Limit:** Claims must be filed within 3 years of the date the wages were due.

**Process:**
1. Complete the Wage Claim form (online or in person)
2. Provide documentation: pay stubs, work schedule, employer contact information
3. Department investigates within 90 days
4. If wage theft is confirmed, employer is ordered to pay back wages + 100% liquidated damages + filing fee

**Common Wage Violations:**
- Failure to pay overtime (1.5x regular rate for hours over 40/week)
- Illegal deductions from wages
- Failure to pay final paycheck within 72 hours of termination (48 hours if employee resigned with notice)
- Tip pooling arrangements that include non-tipped employees

## Paid Leave

### Paid Sick Leave (All Employers)

All employers must provide paid sick leave:
- Employers with 10+ employees: 40 hours/year (5 days)
- Employers with fewer than 10 employees: 40 hours/year, of which 40 hours may be unpaid

**Permitted Uses:**
- Employee or family member's illness or preventive care
- Domestic violence, sexual assault, or stalking (employee or family member)
- Public health emergency closure of employer or school

### Family and Medical Leave

City employees and employees of companies with 50+ employees are entitled to 12 weeks of job-protected unpaid leave per year under FMLA for:
- Birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child
- Serious health condition of employee or family member
- Qualifying military exigency

## Inter-Agency Coordination

### Labor + Social Services
Unemployment benefits are integrated with Social Services benefits screening. Workers filing unemployment claims may be automatically screened for SNAP and HEAP eligibility. Cross-referral protocol is documented in Joint Protocol JP-LAB-SS-2023.

### Labor + DMV (Commercial Drivers)
Employers may verify commercial driver license status through the DOL employer portal. CDL violations that result in disqualification are reported to the employer within 5 business days.

### Labor + Public Health (Workplace Health)
Occupational health incidents (chemical exposures, foodborne illness outbreaks in food service) are jointly investigated by the Department of Labor (safety compliance) and the Public Health Department (disease containment). Joint investigation protocol: JP-LAB-PH-2022.
