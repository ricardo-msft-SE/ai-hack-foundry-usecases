## Vaccination Programs

### Routine Vaccination Clinics

**Schedule:** First and third Tuesday of every month

**Locations:**
- North Health Center: 456 Oak Avenue, 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM
- South Health Center: 789 Pine Road, 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM
- Mobile Clinic: Rotates monthly (schedule at health.exampleville.gov/mobile)

**Available Vaccines (no cost, no insurance required):**
- Influenza (seasonal flu) — offered Sept through March
- COVID-19 (all authorized formulations)
- Tdap (Tetanus, Diphtheria, Pertussis)
- MMR (Measles, Mumps, Rubella)
- Hepatitis A & B
- Varicella (Chickenpox)
- HPV (ages 9–26)
- Pneumococcal (for adults 65+ and those with certain conditions)
- Childhood immunization series (DTaP, Polio, Hib, PCV)

**No appointment needed.** Walk-ins welcome. Patients with insurance should bring their card (the clinic will bill insurance to reduce program costs, but no one is turned away for lack of insurance).

## Food Safety Regulations

### Restaurant and Food Service Permits

All food service establishments must hold a valid City Food Service Permit, renewed annually by March 31.

**Permit Fees:**
| Category | Annual Fee |
|---|---|
| Food cart / mobile unit | $150 |
| Small restaurant (under 50 seats) | $250 |
| Large restaurant (50+ seats) | $400 |
| Food manufacturer / processor | $600 |

### Inspection Process

Establishments are inspected unannounced at least once per year. High-risk facilities (large restaurants, food manufacturers) are inspected twice per year.

**Inspection Scores:**
- **A (90–100):** Compliant — must be posted visibly
- **B (80–89):** Needs improvement — re-inspection required within 30 days
- **C (70–79):** Significant violations — re-inspection required within 2 weeks
- **Critical violations:** May result in immediate closure order

**Common Critical Violations:**
- Food held at improper temperatures
- Poor personal hygiene (no handwashing, sick employees working)
- Evidence of rodents or insects
- Cross-contamination between raw and ready-to-eat foods

### Reporting a Foodborne Illness

To report a suspected foodborne illness outbreak:
- Call: (555) 678-9012 (24-hour line)
- Online: health.exampleville.gov/report-illness

Outbreaks involving 2+ cases from the same source are investigated within 24 hours.

## Environmental Health

### Lead Paint

All buildings constructed before 1978 are presumed to contain lead-based paint.

**Disclosure Requirements:**
- Landlords must disclose known lead paint presence to tenants
- Must provide EPA lead paint pamphlet at signing
- Must disclose any lead paint reports on file

**Lead Testing for Children:**
- All children must be blood-lead tested at ages 1 and 2
- Children 3–6 who haven't been tested should be tested
- Testing is available free at all City Health Centers

**Remediation:**
- If blood lead level ≥ 5 μg/dL, the Health Department will inspect the child's home
- Property owners are legally required to remediate identified lead hazards
- Lead abatement contractors must be city-certified

### Air Quality

**Air Quality Index (AQI) Advisories:**
- AQI < 100: Safe for most people
- AQI 101–150 (Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups): At-risk populations should reduce outdoor activity
- AQI 151–200 (Unhealthy): All people should reduce prolonged outdoor exertion
- AQI > 200: Avoid outdoor activity; health centers extend hours

Real-time AQI: health.exampleville.gov/air-quality

## Communicable Disease Reporting

### Mandatory Reporting (Providers and Labs)

Healthcare providers and laboratories must report the following conditions to the Department of Public Health within stated timeframes:

**Immediately (within 24 hours):**
- Anthrax, Botulism, Cholera, Novel influenza, Plague, Rabies, Smallpox, Viral hemorrhagic fevers

**Within 3 business days:**
- COVID-19, Hepatitis A & B, HIV, Measles, Meningococcal disease, Salmonella, Shigella, Tuberculosis (TB), Whooping cough

**Annually:**
- Lyme disease, Cryptosporidiosis, Giardia

### TB Control Program

- Suspected TB cases must be reported immediately
- Public Health will provide contact tracing within 24 hours
- Free TB testing available at all health centers
- Directly Observed Therapy (DOT) provided for active TB cases
- Close contacts will be identified and offered prophylactic treatment

## Medicaid and Health Insurance

### City-Administered Medicaid

Medicaid provides free or low-cost health insurance for eligible residents. The City Health Department co-administers enrollment with the State Department of Health.

**Eligibility (2024):**
| Category | Income Limit |
|---|---|
| Adults (19–64) | 138% FPL |
| Children (under 19) | 218% FPL |
| Pregnant women | 213% FPL |
| People with disabilities | Evaluated case-by-case |

**How to Apply:**
- Online: health.exampleville.gov/medicaid
- By phone: (555) 789-0123
- In person: Any City Health Center

**Processing time:** 45 days (10 days for emergency Medicaid)

### Child Health Plus

Children under 19 who do not qualify for Medicaid but whose family income is at or below 400% FPL may qualify for subsidized Child Health Plus coverage. Monthly premiums range from $0–$60 depending on income.

## Inter-Agency Protocols

### Public Health + Social Services
Co-enrollment: Medicaid applications processed jointly with SNAP and other benefits at the same intake. Staff can access the Joint Enrollment Portal (JEP) at portal.exampleville.gov.

### Public Health + Department of Labor
Workplace illness outbreaks (food service, industrial) are jointly investigated. Immediate notification protocol: if DOL identifies 3+ workers ill from same workplace, they notify Public Health within 2 hours. Protocol JP-LAB-PH-2022.

### Public Health + Emergency Management (OEM)
Mass vaccination and medication dispensing plans are maintained by the Health Department and integrated into the City Emergency Operations Plan (EOP). Points of Dispensing (PODs) can be activated within 12 hours of OEM request.
