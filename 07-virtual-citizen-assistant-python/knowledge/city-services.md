## What to Bring — By Service

### Building Inspection
- **Bring:** Building permit number, site plan (approved copy), contact information for contractor on site
- **Location:** Inspector comes to your property — provide the property address when booking
- **Note:** Someone 18+ must be present at the property during the inspection

### Benefits Counseling (SNAP / HEAP / TANF)
- **Bring:**
  - Photo ID (government issued)
  - Proof of address (utility bill or lease, dated within 60 days)
  - Proof of income (pay stubs for last 30 days, or proof of no income)
  - Social Security numbers for all household members
  - Bank account information (optional — for direct deposit setup)
- **Location:** Social Services offices — North, South, East, West, or Downtown

### Business License — New Application
- **Bring:**
  - Federal EIN or SSN (for sole proprietors)
  - Business address confirmation (lease or deed)
  - Zoning compliance confirmation (obtain at zoning.exampleville.gov before appointment)
  - Business owner government-issued ID
- **Location:** City Hall Business Office or any district office

### DMV — ID Renewal
- **Bring:**
  - Current ID (even if expired)
  - Proof of residency (2 documents: utility bill, lease, bank statement)
  - Social Security card or proof of Social Security number
  - Birth certificate or passport
- **Location:** City-operated DMV offices (separate from state DMV)

### DMV — Title Transfer
- **Bring:**
  - Current vehicle title (signed over by seller)
  - Completed title transfer form (download at dmv.exampleville.gov)
  - Bill of sale
  - Payment for title transfer fee ($50) and sales tax (if applicable)
  - Valid ID

### Housing Assistance Intake
- **Bring:**
  - Government-issued photo ID for all adult household members
  - Proof of residency (current lease or mortgage statement)
  - Proof of income for all household members
  - Most recent bank statements (2 months)
  - Proof of hardship (eviction notice, late payment notice, employer layoff letter)
- **Location:** Housing Services office — 100 Main Street, Downtown

### Zoning Consultation
- **Bring:**
  - Property address and parcel ID (find at gis.exampleville.gov)
  - Proposed use description (brief written summary of your project)
  - Site plan (preliminary is acceptable — not required to be stamped)
- **Location:** Zoning Division, City Hall

### Food Service Permit Pre-Inspection
- **Bring:**
  - Facility layout drawing (kitchen and food service areas)
  - Equipment list (make, model, and placement)
  - Food manager certification (ServSafe or equivalent)
  - Copy of lease or deed for the premises
- **Location:** Inspector comes to your establishment — provide address when booking

## Benefits Programs

### SNAP (Food Assistance)
- For households with income at or below 130% of the Federal Poverty Level
- Apply at: benefits.exampleville.gov or in person with appointment
- Processing time: 30 days standard, 7 days expedited
- Benefits loaded to EBT card

### HEAP (Utility Assistance)
- For households with income at or below 60% of State Median Income
- Helps pay heating, cooling, and energy bills
- Apply at: benefits.exampleville.gov
- Apply early (program has limited annual funding — first come, first served)

### Emergency Rental Assistance
- For renters at risk of eviction with income at or below 80% AMI
- Apply at: housing.exampleville.gov/rental-assistance
- Processing: 30 days standard, 7 days emergency

## Building and Permits

### Building Permit Process Summary
1. Apply online at permits.exampleville.gov
2. Plan review: 5–15 business days
3. Permit issued digitally — display at job site
4. Schedule required inspections as work progresses
5. Final inspection → Certificate of Occupancy issued

### Permit Types That Don't Require a Permit
- Cosmetic renovations (paint, flooring, cabinets — no structural changes)
- Replacing fixtures in the same location
- Fences under 4 feet in residential zones

## 311 — General City Services

For everything not covered above, contact 311:
- **Phone:** Dial 311 (local calls only) or (555) 100-0311
- **Online:** 311.exampleville.gov
- **App:** Exampleville 311 (iOS and Android)

311 handles: pothole reports, graffiti removal, noise complaints, missed trash pickup, street light outages, and general questions about city services.

**Non-emergency animal control:** (555) 200-0311
**Non-emergency police:** (555) 300-0311
**Emergency:** 911
